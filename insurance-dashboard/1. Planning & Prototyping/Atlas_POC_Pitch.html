Why now

Five ways this shows up on our desks every quarter

None of this is hypothetical — it's the Section 2 problem statement from the PRD, restated as what it actually costs the team in time and risk.

01

Fragmented data

Policy terms sit in PDFs, broker slips, emails, and local spreadsheets — no common schema, no single place to look.

02

Invisible under-insurance

No systematic comparison of sums insured against value or PML, so gaps surface only when a claim exposes them.

03

Manual, slow capture

Extracting key terms is manual and error-prone — and doesn't scale across renewals or new acquisitions.

04

Renewal & lapse risk

Expiries tracked locally, entity by entity. A missed renewal can leave an asset genuinely uninsured.

05

Limited assurance

Auditors, the Board, and regulators expect evidence of adequate cover — assembled manually, every single cycle.

Most of what's above is deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same ratio, score, or status, and every one of them shows its lineage. AI only makes a judgement call in two places: reading a messy source document, and the news assistant (V2). That split is deliberate, not a limitation — it's what makes the Auditor row above possible.
The actual ask

A small, bounded POC — not a commitment to build the whole thing

Scoped to prove the two riskiest assumptions — extraction accuracy and whether the ratios actually hold up against a manual check — before anyone signs off on a full build.

Scope

In scope for the POC

  • ✓1–2 pilot entities (proposed: Keppel Infrastructure & one higher-complexity site)
  • ✓Document ingestion on a real sample of broker/policy documents
  • ✓Core ratios: ITV, Coverage Gap %, Renewal Pipeline (§7.1, §7.6)
  • ✓Group Overview + Risk Hotspot views only
  • ✓Human-in-the-loop validation, exactly as specified in §9

Explicitly out of scope

  • –News & sector risk intelligence (V2 capability)
  • –Full Group rollout across all entities
  • –Automated alerting and configurable risk weights
  • –Any integration beyond a sample data feed
  • –Production go-live decision — that's a separate gate
