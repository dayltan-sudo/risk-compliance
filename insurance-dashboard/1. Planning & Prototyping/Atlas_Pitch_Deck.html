The problem

Today, no one can answer “how well is the Group insured?” quickly, or with confidence.

Our programme spans many insurance lines, dozens of legal entities, and operations across multiple countries — but the data describing it is not consolidated, standardised, or continuously monitored.

01Fragmented dataPolicy terms live in PDFs, broker slips, emails, and local spreadsheets — no common schema, no single repository.
02Invisible under-insuranceNo systematic comparison of sums insured against value or PML, so under-covered assets surface only when a claim exposes the gap.
03Manual, slow captureExtracting key terms from documents is manual and error-prone — and does not scale across renewals or new acquisitions.
04No risk-prioritised viewThe Group cannot easily see which countries, sites, or entities carry the highest uninsured or under-insured exposure.
05Renewal & lapse risk, limited assuranceExpiries are tracked locally — a missed renewal can leave an asset uninsured — while auditors and the Board expect evidence assembled by hand each cycle.

What it does

Six core capabilities, one source of truth.

01 / DASHBOARD
Interactive global dashboard
Group-to-policy drill-down, world map, and KPI tiles — the whole programme on one screen.
02 / RATIO ENGINE
Coverage & risk ratio engine
FX-normalised KPIs, recalculated automatically, with full calculation lineage for audit.
03 / INGESTION
Document ingestion
Reads slips, wordings, schedules, and endorsements — with human-in-the-loop validation before anything posts.
04 / HOTSPOTS
Risk-hotspot scoring
A tunable composite score ranks the highest-risk, under-covered countries, sites, and entities.
05 / FILTERS
Multi-select filtering
Toggle any insurance line or dimension and the whole dashboard focuses on the slice you care about.
06 / INTELLIGENCE · V2
News & sector risk intelligence
Monitors the sectors and geographies Keppel invests in to pre-emptively flag emerging risk.

Only two things in this platform are AI making a judgement call — document extraction and the news assistant. The ratios and risk score are deterministic and reproducible, the way a number in an audit pack has to be.
