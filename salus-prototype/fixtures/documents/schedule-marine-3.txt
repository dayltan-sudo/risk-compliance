EXCLUSIONS
Institute Cargo Clauses (A) exclusions apply in full: wilful misconduct of the
insured; ordinary leakage, loss in weight or volume, or ordinary wear and tear;
insufficient or unsuitable packing; inherent vice; delay; insolvency or financial
default of the carrier; use of any atomic or nuclear weapon; unseaworthiness or
unfitness of the vessel where the insured had knowledge at the time of loading.
Additional exclusions: war and strikes (separate cover required); cyber loss;
loss of market; fines and penalties.

CONDITIONS

Subject to the Institute Cargo Clauses (A) 1/1/2009 and the Harbourline marine
cargo wording HBL-MC-W-2025-v1. Claims payable in Singapore in USD.
