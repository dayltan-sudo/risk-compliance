KESTREL UNDERWRITING ASIA PTE. LTD.
CYBER AND TECHNOLOGY LIABILITY POLICY WORDING
Form KUA-CYBER-2025-v3

1. INSURING CLAUSE
The Insurer agrees to indemnify the Insured for direct loss arising from a
Network Security Failure, a Privacy Breach, or a Technology Error, occurring
during the Period of Insurance and discovered during the Period of Insurance or
within 90 days thereafter.

2. DEFINITIONS
Network Security Failure means unauthorised access to, or unauthorised use of, a
Computer System that results in a disruption or a loss.
Privacy Breach means the accidental or deliberate release of Personal Data.
Technology Error means an error, omission or defect in the Insured's Technology
Products or Technology Services.
Computer System includes hardware, software, firmware, networks and cloud
services used by the Insured or by a service provider on the Insured's behalf.

[[page 2]]

3. LIMITS OF LIABILITY
The Limit of Liability for any one claim and in the aggregate is the amount
stated in the Schedule. The Aggregate Limit of Liability is the amount stated in
the Schedule and is the maximum payable for all claims in the Period of
Insurance, inclusive of Defence Costs.
Each claim is subject to the Deductible stated in the Schedule.
Sublimits stated in the Schedule apply within, and not in addition to, the Limit
of Liability.

4. EXCLUSIONS
This policy does not cover: system failure without a malicious or accidental
external cause; loss arising from unencrypted portable devices or removable
media; loss arising from the Insured's failure to apply a critical security
patch within 30 days of release; contractual penalties and liquidated damages;
intellectual property infringement; bodily injury, property damage or pollution;
terrorism, war and nuclear risks; cryptocurrency mining and theft of
cryptocurrency; loss of market, loss of reputation not consequent on a covered
Privacy Breach, and fines except where insurable by law.

5. CONDITIONS
5.1 The Insured must maintain the security controls warranted in the Schedule.
5.2 Claims notification within 72 hours of discovery, in writing.
5.3 The Insurer may appoint an independent forensic investigator.
5.4 Defence Costs are payable within the Limit of Liability and reduce it.
5.5 Singapore law governs this policy. Disputes are referred to the Singapore
    International Arbitration Centre.
5.6 Cancellation: 30 days notice by either party, pro rata premium.

6. SCHEDULE
The Schedule attached to this wording records the Insured, the Period of
Insurance, the Limit of Liability, the Aggregate Limit of Liability, the
Deductible, the Sublimits, the Premium and the applicable GST.
