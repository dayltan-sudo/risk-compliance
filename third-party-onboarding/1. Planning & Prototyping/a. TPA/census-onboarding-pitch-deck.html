THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

Built to avoid automation bias.

Review, not create

It suggests. The human commits.

No fabrication

Judgment fields left blank + "needs confirmation" — never an inferred guess.

Honest handoff

Never implies a task is approved, submitted, or a hit cleared.

Evidence, not verdicts

Shows the source and lets the human decide. No pass/fail column.

THE TOOL SPEEDS THE CHECK. IT DOES NOT REPLACE THE JUDGMENT.
